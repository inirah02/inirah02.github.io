About.

I'm a software developer, researcher, and technical writer working across AI infrastructure, data systems, and clinical AI.

By day, I'm at IBM in the Data & AI division, contributing to watsonx.data — a hybrid open data lakehouse purpose-built for AI workloads. I've worked on cloud cost optimization, internal Kubernetes tooling in Go, FedRAMP compliance, the OpenSearch acquisition tech preview, and a patent project on agentic AI. I also lead the Speaking & Authoring Working Group under IBM ISL's Core-X initiative.

In parallel, I'm a remote Undergraduate Researcher in Software Engineering at UIUC under Prof. Darko Marinov (MIR Group), working on fairness testing in ML systems and automated program repair. I'm also a Volunteer Research Associate at Georgia Tech's Center for the Study of Systems Biology and a Biomedical AI Researcher at Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth, working on personalized digital health models for schizophrenia.

Earlier, I interned at Niramai Health Analytix on breast-cancer screening tools, did research at IIT Hyderabad on gene regulatory networks via PySCENIC, and built Project ROOTS — a cognitive-games app for delaying dementia onset that won the Cisco ThingQbator national cohort and reached 350+ users.

I led HSP, the largest FOSS community at PES University, started Project Expo, and was a core member of GDSC and ACM-W chapters. I've taught Generative AI as a TA, mentored through Sitara Akka, and given talks at KubeCon, IndiaFOSS, Women Who Go, Apache Beam @ Google, WHPC Purdue, and others.

I write at the intersection of technology and accessibility — swiftieintech, my newsletter that uses Taylor Swift's discography as a thematic frame for technical concepts, and Breakdown the BMC: AI Innovator's Playbook, a biweekly series with Aishwarya Srinivasan analyzing AI startups. I was named a LinkedIn Top Voice in Data Science.
